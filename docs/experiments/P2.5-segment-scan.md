# P2.5 scan — WR segment times vs MrWint's enemy-free optima, and the 8-4 speed profile

Question: where on the route is the WR *not* at the enemy-free movement bound? Those places are room that a
player-only search could find; everywhere else only enemy interactions (bounces, shells) or glitches can gain.

## Method
`third_party/smb-opt/src/case/w*.rs` documents MrWint's exhaustive segment solutions (start state, goal, input
length). For each case on the warps route the WR's time for the same segment was read from the FCEUX dump
(`data/wr/fceux_wr.ram`, `tools/ram_trace.py`): start = the WR row in the case's start state (control frame
`GameEngineSubroutine` 8 at the room's fixed entry x, or the row at the case's start x), end = the first row
satisfying the case's goal (x threshold, or pipe entry `GameEngineSubroutine` 3). Scratch script in the session
log; traces regenerable with `tools/ram_trace.py data/wr/fceux_wr.ram 15224 17868 GameEngineSubroutine AreaPointer
Player_PageLoc Player_X_Position Player_X_MoveForce Player_Y_HighPos Player_Y_Position Player_Y_Speed Player_X_Speed …`.

## 8-4 speed profile (F67) — the unquantized level
`Player_X_Speed` per frame between the solved segments: room 1 middle (x ≥ 187 → pipe entry, 445 frames, includes
the 4-page loop-back, net 89 px = 1113 px travelled) **40 on every frame**; room 2 middle (157 frames, 392 px) 40 on
every frame; room 3 middle (51 frames, 128 px) 40 on every frame. Water room (control → side-pipe entry, 696 frames,
1020 px): swim cap 24 on 667 frames, the other 29 are the acceleration from 0 at the entry (rows 16720–16748).
Bowser room (control → axe, 278 frames, 653 px): 40 on 246 frames, 32 below (the acceleration from the pipe exit).
So 8-4 is at the movement bound everywhere except the two fixed accelerations; its remaining levers are the
pipe/area transitions, the water-room end alignment, Bowser/RNG interactions, and glitches — not running.

## Conclusion
On every route segment MrWint solved, HappyLee's 2011 inputs are already optimal for player-only movement, and
8-4's unsolved sections run at the cap. Player-only search can therefore only pay off where (a) a segment is not
yet at a *known* bound — 4-1, 4-2 (HappyLee's own top route is "2 frames short", F36), 8-1, 8-2, 8-3 have no
exhaustive segment optima at all — or (b) the bound itself moves through enemy interactions or glitches.
Reprioritised in STATUS: 4-2 top route (a 2-frame framerule target, running question) ahead of the room-1
enemy-model port (H29 has a low prior: a stomp gives a fixed y speed −4, no A-boost, so bounces add no height).
