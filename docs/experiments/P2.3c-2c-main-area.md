## Question
`W42Main`'s bound is MrWint's x-only table (`XPosHeuristic`): 540 steps at the root (record 6584) against the WR's
588 and the framerule target 575 (= 588 − 13). A proof run with 35 frames of slack is hopeless (the frontier doubles
per layer, see below), so the main-area search needs a staged bound like the warp zone's (P2.3c-2). To design it:
(1) where along the WR is the x-only bound loose? (2) what does a *top* route cost in the model — HappyLee says 577
(F36), i.e. 37 above the x-bound; is that physics coupling the bound must learn, or something outside the model?

## Tool change: `bfscx --goal-x PX [--goal-y PY]` (position goals for segment searches)
`SearchGoal::steps_until_x_at_least` (new trait method; `W42Main` returns its x-table value) replaces the case's bound,
and "x_pos ≥ PX (and Player_Y ≤ PY) after a Success step" replaces the goal. Among the goal layer's goals the printed
one is the best by (x speed, x) so chained segments start from the strongest arrival. Reconstruction (`bfscx-path`)
is unchanged (parent record + input). Regression: the 4-2 WR-suffix control (`… 6584 575 587 --lift 0 --check-path 12`)
reproduces its counts exactly (6, 16, 34, 70, 134, 673, 3472, 16472, 69489, 257001; goal at layer 10, 16,194
transitions, 20.8 s; `runs/P2.3c/regr_w42main_p575_d587.log`). `tools/smb-opt-modes.patch` regenerated.
**Frontier law for position goals**: with the deadline 28 frames above the x-bound (S1, deadline 165, root 137) the
frontier doubled every layer (2.8M at layer 40, `runs/P2.3c/top_s1_x339_y112_d165.log`, killed) — a position-goal
BFS is only cheap when the deadline is at the segment optimum, hence `tools/bfscx_ladder.sh` (deadlines tight-first).
